# Community conduct

We want technical discussion to be safe, focused and welcoming.

- Be respectful and critique work rather than people.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or expose private information about others.
- Treat security reports and sensitive user data as confidential.
- Give credit for upstream and community work.
- Keep discussions relevant and provide reproducible evidence where possible.
- Maintainers may edit, hide or remove abusive or unsafe content and may restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Do not use a
public issue when doing so could expose a person or a security vulnerability.
Until a named community team is established, the repository owner is
responsible for enforcement.
